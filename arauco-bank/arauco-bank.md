# Arauco Bank

**Arauco Bank**, at the top of **Arauco Chain**, will be the first neobank built completely on-chain with a B2B focus, so that companies of all sizes and from all parts of the world can carry out local and international transactions in a simple, economical, and immediate way.

**The first version will be available in the second half of 2025.**
